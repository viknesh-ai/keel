# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is a technical collaboration. Participate accordingly:

- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume competence and good faith in others.
- Be direct. Disagreement expressed clearly is a contribution; passive hostility is not.
- Accept that maintainers make final calls on scope and design, and that "no" is a complete answer.

## Unacceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, publishing
others' private information, and sustained disruption of technical discussion.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers via the contact address in `SECURITY.md`.
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently
ban any participant, and will explain the reason to the person concerned.

This is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant, shortened deliberately.
