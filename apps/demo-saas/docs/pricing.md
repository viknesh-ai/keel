# Pricing

_Last reviewed: 12 August 2026. Owner: Revenue Operations._

List pricing for Northwind Cloud. Sales may discount Enterprise; Free through
Pro are the same price for everyone, and support should not imply otherwise.

## Monthly

| Plan | India (INR) | International (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Free | ₹0 | $0 |
| Starter | ₹980 | $49 |
| Pro | ₹4,980 | $249 |
| Enterprise | From ₹19,980 | From $999 |

## Annual

Billed once up front, about 17% below twelve months at the monthly rate.

| Plan | India (INR/year) | International (USD/year) |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | ₹9,800 | $490 |
| Pro | ₹49,800 | $2,490 |
| Enterprise | Negotiated | Negotiated |

## Why INR is not a converted USD price

Indian pricing is set for the Indian market, not derived from the dollar price
at the day's exchange rate. The two move independently, and a customer who
notices the ratio is not 1:83 has not found a bug.

This also means we do not offer to switch a customer's billing currency to get a
better rate. Currency follows the billing address.

## Overages

| Metric | Included | Overage |
|---|---|---|
| API calls | Per plan, above | ₹0.02 / $0.0002 per call |
| Seats | Per plan, above | Prorated plan seat rate |
| Storage | 50 GB (Pro), 10 GB (Starter) | ₹8 / $0.10 per GB / month |
| Exports | Unlimited | — |

Overages appear on the following invoice. They are not charged in real time, and
a customer will not see a card charge the moment they exceed a limit.

## Taxes

Prices exclude tax. Indian customers are charged 18% GST. EU customers are
charged VAT at their local rate unless they supply a valid VAT number, in which
case the reverse charge applies. US customers may be charged state sales tax
depending on their address.

Tax is calculated at invoice time from the billing address on file, so a
customer who moves should update it before the next renewal rather than after.

## Currency support

Northwind Cloud bills in INR, USD, EUR and GBP. Currency is fixed at signup from
the billing address and does not change with a later address update — changing it
means a new subscription, because the old one's price is denominated in the old
currency.
