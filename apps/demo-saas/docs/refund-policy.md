# Refund policy

_Last reviewed: 12 August 2026. Owner: Finance._

This policy covers refunds on Northwind Cloud subscriptions and one-off orders.
It is the document support staff should quote from, and it is deliberately
specific about the cases where the answer is "no", because a policy that only
describes the happy path is not a policy.

## Subscriptions

### Monthly plans

A monthly subscription can be refunded in full within **7 days** of the charge,
once per customer per calendar year. After 7 days the charge stands, and
cancelling stops the next renewal rather than reversing the last one.

The 7-day window runs from the charge date shown on the invoice, not from the
date the customer noticed it.

### Annual plans

Annual plans are **not refunded pro rata mid-term**. This is the case support
gets asked about most, and the answer surprises people, so state it plainly and
early in the conversation.

What we do instead, in order of preference:

1. **Credit toward the next term.** The unused months are applied as account
   credit at the next renewal. This is the default offer and needs no approval.
2. **Downgrade with credit.** If the customer wants to stay but at a smaller
   plan, the difference becomes credit. Also no approval needed.
3. **Cash refund of unused months.** Requires Finance approval and is granted
   only where Northwind has materially failed to deliver — sustained downtime,
   a billing error on our side, or a feature removed mid-term that the customer
   bought the plan for.

A customer being unhappy is not, by itself, case 3. A customer who was billed
for a plan they did not agree to is.

### Trials

Trials are never charged, so there is nothing to refund. If a trial converted to
a paid plan because the customer missed the reminder, treat it as a monthly
7-day refund even if the plan is annual. This is an explicit exception and it is
intentional: the customer did not make a deliberate annual commitment.

## One-off orders

Orders are refundable within **30 days** if the item has not been fulfilled.
Once an order shows `fulfilled`, refunds are at Finance's discretion and are
normally declined for digital deliverables, because the value has already
transferred.

A `cancelled` order was never charged and needs no refund. Support sometimes
confuses `cancelled` with `refunded`; they are different states and the customer
sees the difference on their statement.

## Currency and timing

Refunds are issued in the **original currency of the charge**. We do not convert.
A customer charged in INR is refunded in INR, even if they now prefer USD, and
the amount is the amount charged — exchange rate movement between the charge and
the refund is not something we absorb or pass on.

Card refunds take 5–10 business days to appear, and that delay is the issuing
bank's, not ours. Say so; it prevents a second contact a week later.

## What support can do without approval

| Action | Approval |
|---|---|
| Monthly refund inside 7 days | None |
| Annual credit toward next term | None |
| Downgrade with credit | None |
| Order refund, unfulfilled, inside 30 days | None |
| Annual cash refund | Finance |
| Order refund, fulfilled | Finance |
| Any refund over ₹200,000 or equivalent | Finance |

## Escalation

If a customer disputes a decision under this policy, escalate to Finance rather
than re-deciding. Two staff members giving different answers to the same
question is worse for the customer than a slow answer, and it is how we end up
with a policy nobody trusts.
